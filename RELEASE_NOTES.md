Suzuka’s Better Shooting v1.0.0

First full release, consolidating the preview features under **Suzuka Better Shooting**.

- Automatic full-charge cycling with weapon toggles.
- Held three-round burst repeat with adjustable pause.
- Optional semi-auto held-fire repeat; rate factor 0–1 in steps of 0.05. OFF restores manual fire and resets the factor to 1.
- APW-1 crosshair and Senator/Talon normal crosshair switches.
- Senator fixed native laser sight; Talon default laser/flashlight.
- Independent PISTOL STAT BOOST toggle: ergonomics +10 and two sway multipliers 1→0.85. OFF restores native values without disabling crosshairs or lasers.

Requires Bingus Shared Loader API 1 and Mod Options Menu for settings. Replace older AIO and overlapping standalone components, deploy with your mod manager, then restart. Re-equip the Senator to recreate attachments. Its fixed laser replaces the selectable flashlight.

Offline LuaJIT, component write/readback/restoration, all eight crosshair/stat combinations and all 16 installer combinations passed. The fixed Senator laser and full release combination still need in-game confirmation.
